Our purpose

Supporting better wheat planning with weather insights

GrainCast helps growers compare wheat-growing regions using short-term forecasts and long-term weather patterns. By bringing together the upcoming weather conditions and historical climate data, we aim to help farmers make well informed decisions around crop planning.

Key visualisations

Understanding each section

The Map section shows the available weather stations, of which you will be able to select and compare growing locations side by side.

The Predictions section provides a 7-day outlook and personalised recommendation for your selected location and wheat variety.

The Historical Data section allows you to compare rainfall, wind speed, or temperature conditions between locations across your chosen date range.
